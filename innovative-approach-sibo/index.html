Our Process

Root-Cause Functional Gut Support

1. Find

You have to do a thorough investigation to uncover the root cause. Things do not go awry without a reason. If you do not find the primary DRIVER, the DYSFUNCTION will continue. The driver is not the same for everyone, neither is there a set protocol that will work for everyone. And gut issues are varied. I see many who suffer from SIBO (small intestine bacterial overgrowth), IBS (irritable bowel syndrome) and other gut related issues.

At my Springfield office, I take the time to take thorough health history, go over lab work (order if necessary), utilize a technique called Systems Health Care to narrow the problem down further. There is always a primary driver to dysfunction in the body, it does NOT randomly appear for no reason.

2. Support

Fixing gut issues such as SIBO and IBS require specific dietary modifications and herbal supplementation to promote a healthy gut microbiome. A broad approach often misses the mark. I will do my best to make sure there is no stone left unturned; finding the specific solution to your issue. That is why it is important to take a holistic approach and account for every component that is driving the issues. Check out the free ebook above for even more info!
