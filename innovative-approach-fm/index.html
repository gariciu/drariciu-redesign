The Process

Here’s how we uncover and support the root causes of chronic symptoms:

1. Find 🔍

We start by uncovering the root causes behind your symptoms. Through a thorough health history, advanced functional lab testing, and Systems Health Care muscle testing, we look beyond symptoms to identify the underlying structural, chemical, and mental patterns driving dysfunction.

✅ Goal: Discover what’s keeping your body from healing on its own.

2. Support 🛠️

Once we know what’s driving the dysfunction, we create a personalized plan to support your body’s natural healing ability. This may include nutritional guidance, lifestyle adjustments, targeted supplementation, and myofascial or structural care.

✅ Goal: Support your body’s natural ability to heal and restore function.
